Egusi Soup

Description

Egusi Soup is a rich and flavorful Nigerian soup made from ground melon seeds cooked with leafy vegetables, peppers, palm oil, and a variety of meat or fish. It has a thick, hearty texture and a distinctive nutty flavor. Egusi Soup is commonly served with Nigerian swallows such as pounded yam, eba, fufu, or semolina.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups ground egusi (melon seeds)
  • 1/2 cup palm oil
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 3 medium tomatoes
  • 2 red bell peppers
  • 2-3 Scotch bonnet peppers, to taste
  • 500 g assorted meat, beef, or goat meat
  • 300 g stockfish or dried fish, soaked and cleaned
  • 200 g smoked or dried fish, cleaned and deboned
  • 3 cups chopped spinach, ugu, or bitterleaf
  • 2 tablespoons ground crayfish
  • 2 seasoning cubes
  • 1 teaspoon ground uziza seeds (optional)
  • 1-2 cups meat stock or water
  • Salt, to taste

Steps

  1. Wash the meat thoroughly and place it in a large pot. Season with salt, chopped onion, and one seasoning cube.
  2. Add a small amount of water and cook over medium heat until the meat is tender. Reserve the resulting stock.
  3. If using stockfish, soak it in warm water until softened, then clean and remove any bones. Clean and debone the smoked or dried fish.
  4. Blend the tomatoes, red bell peppers, Scotch bonnet peppers, and remaining onion into a smooth mixture.
  5. Heat the palm oil in a large pot over medium heat. Do not allow the oil to smoke.
  6. Add the chopped onion and sauté for 2-3 minutes until softened.
  7. Pour in the blended pepper mixture and cook for 10-15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the mixture thickens and excess water reduces.
  8. Add the ground egusi and stir thoroughly. Cook for 5-8 minutes, stirring frequently to prevent the egusi from sticking to the bottom of the pot.
  9. Gradually add the meat stock while stirring until the soup reaches your desired consistency.
  10. Add the cooked meat, stockfish, smoked fish, and ground crayfish. Stir gently to combine.
  11. Cover and simmer over low heat for 10-15 minutes, allowing the egusi to cook thoroughly and absorb the flavors.
  12. Add the chopped leafy vegetables and stir gently. Cook for another 3-5 minutes, keeping the vegetables slightly vibrant and tender.
  13. Taste and adjust the seasoning and salt as needed.
  14. Remove from the heat and allow the soup to rest for a few minutes before serving.
