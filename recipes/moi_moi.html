Moi Moi

Description

Moi Moi is a traditional Nigerian steamed bean pudding made from peeled black-eyed peas or brown beans blended with peppers, onions, and seasonings. It has a smooth, soft texture and a rich, savory flavor that makes it a popular side dish or main meal. Moi Moi is commonly enjoyed with jollof rice, pap, custard, bread, or eaten on its own as a nutritious, protein-rich dish.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups dried black-eyed peas or brown beans
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 2 red bell peppers, chopped
  • 2 Scotch bonnet peppers (adjust to taste)
  • 1/2 cup vegetable oil
  • 2 cups warm fish, chicken, or vegetable stock (or water)
  • 2 seasoning cubes
  • 1 teaspoon ground crayfish (optional)
  • 1 teaspoon curry powder (optional)
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground nutmeg (optional)
  • Salt, to taste
  • 2 hard-boiled eggs, sliced (optional)
  • 1 cup cooked flaked fish, corned beef, or cooked shredded chicken (optional)

Steps

  1. Soak the beans in water for 30 minutes to 1 hour. Rub the beans between your hands to remove the skins, rinsing several times until the skins float away. Drain the peeled beans.
  2. Blend the peeled beans with the onion, red bell peppers, Scotch bonnet peppers, and a little stock or water until the mixture is completely smooth.
  3. Pour the blended mixture into a large bowl.
  4. Add the vegetable oil, remaining stock, seasoning cubes, crayfish, curry powder, nutmeg, and salt. Mix thoroughly until the batter is smooth and well combined.
  5. Taste the batter and adjust the seasoning if necessary.
  6. Lightly grease Moi Moi containers, ramekins, foil cups, or banana leaves. Fill each container about three-quarters full.
  7. If desired, add slices of hard-boiled egg or portions of cooked fish, corned beef, or shredded chicken into each filled container.
  8. Arrange the containers in a large pot with a rack or folded cloth at the bottom. Carefully pour hot water into the pot until it reaches about one-third of the height of the containers.
  9. Cover the containers and the pot tightly. Steam over medium heat for 45-60 minutes, adding more hot water if needed during cooking.
  10. Check for doneness by inserting a toothpick or skewer into the center. If it comes out clean, the Moi Moi is cooked.
  11. Remove from the steamer and allow to cool slightly before serving.
